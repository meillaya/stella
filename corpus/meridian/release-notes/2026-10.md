# Release notes — October 2026

Last updated: 2026-10-30 · Applies to: Meridian v4.5.x, v4.6.0

Dates are production release dates; staged rollouts complete within 48 hours unless noted.

## 2026-10-02 — v4.5.0

**Webhook intake connector (Business and Enterprise):** accepts signed JSON events over HTTPS,
verified with an HMAC-SHA256 signature; see [Connectors](../data-sources/connectors.md).
**Dashboard freshness indicator:** every tile shows the last successful sync time. **Bulk API
export part files:** exports larger than 2 GB are split into numbered part files; see
[Query API](../api/query-api.md). **Fixed:** a timed-out query could hold a concurrency slot for
up to 10 minutes instead of releasing it at the 120-second timeout.

## 2026-10-20 — Billing and retention policy v4.0 announced

**Effective date: 2026-11-01.** This entry announces the current customer-facing policies. From
the effective date it replaces [Billing and refunds](../policies/billing-refunds.md) (**policy
v3.2**, last updated 2026-03-12) and [Data retention](../policies/data-retention.md) (**policy
v3.0**, last updated 2026-02-18). Those pages remain online only as historical references and must
not be quoted for requests, invoices, or deletion timelines dated on or after 2026-11-01. Where any
older page or release note conflicts with the tables below, **the tables below govern**.

### Billing policy v4.0 — refunds and cancellations

Cancellation still takes effect at the end of the current billing period, access continues through
the period already paid, and the 20% annual billing discount is unchanged. Refund windows and fees
change as follows:

| Subscription | Refund window | Refund amount |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Annual, first 14 days after purchase or renewal | Full refund | 100% of the amount paid for the current term |
| Annual, day 15 through day 75 | Partial refund | Unused full months at the effective monthly rate, less a **5% administration fee** |
| Annual, after day 75 | No refund | Service continues to the end of the paid term |
| Monthly, first 5 days after the invoice date | Full refund | 100% of the monthly invoice |
| Monthly, after day 5 | No refund | Service continues to the end of the paid month |

Approved refunds return to the original payment method within **10 business days**. SLA service
credits remain service credits and are not cash refunds; see
[SLA and support tiers](../support/sla-support-tiers.md).

### Retention policy v4.0 — windows and purge timeline

| Data category | Starter | Team | Business | Enterprise |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raw synced record data | 90 days | 18 months | 36 months | 7 years |
| Dashboard history window | 30 days | 12 months | 36 months | Custom |
| Audit logs | 12 months | 12 months | 24 months | 24 months |
| Query result cache | 12 hours | 12 hours | 12 hours | 12 hours |
| Report export files in workspace | 14 days | 14 days | 14 days | 14 days |
| Backup snapshots | 35 days | 35 days | 35 days | 35 days |
| Deleted workspace soft-delete period | 30 days | 30 days | 30 days | 30 days |
| Expired trial workspaces before purge | 30 days | — | — | — |

Other retention rules are unchanged: source-side deletions propagate within 24 hours of the next
successful sync; a deleted workspace can be restored by its Owner during the soft-delete period and
is permanently purged after it; a verified data-purge request completes within 30 days; backup
snapshots expire after 35 days. From the effective date the **newer numbers in this table apply**.

## 2026-10-30 — v4.6.0 rollout

v4.6.0 began rolling out on 2026-10-30 and completed on 2026-10-31. It contains the enforcement
plumbing for policy v4.0 plus API key rotation improvements: rotating a key now allows a
**24-hour overlap** in which the old key still works, so integrations can switch without downtime.
Policy v4.0 takes effect on 2026-11-01.
