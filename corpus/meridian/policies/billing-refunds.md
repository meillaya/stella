# Billing and refunds

**Status: SUPERSEDED.** This page documents **billing policy v3.2**, which applied from
2025-04-01 through 2026-10-31. It is retained for invoice reconciliation and historical
reference only.

> **Do not quote these terms for requests made on or after 2026-11-01.** Billing policy **v4.0**
> took effect on 2026-11-01 and is published in
> [Release notes, October 2026](../release-notes/2026-10.md). Where this page and that release
> note disagree, the release note governs.

Last updated: 2026-03-12 · Applies to: Meridian v3.x · Policy version: 3.2

## Cancellation

Cancel any time from **Settings → Billing → Cancel plan**. Cancellation takes effect at the **end
of the current billing period**; access continues through the period already paid. Cancelling does
not delete data automatically; data handling is described in [Data retention](data-retention.md).

## Refund policy v3.2 (historical)

This table applied to refund requests submitted before 2026-11-01.

| Subscription | Refund window | Refund amount |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Annual, first 30 days after purchase or renewal | Full refund | 100% of the amount paid for the current term |
| Annual, day 31 through day 90 | Partial refund | Unused full months at the effective monthly rate, less a **12% administration fee** |
| Annual, after day 90 | No refund | Service continues to the end of the paid term |
| Monthly, first 14 days after the first invoice of the subscription | Full refund | 100% of the first monthly invoice |
| Monthly, after day 14 | No refund | Service continues to the end of the paid month |

"Unused full months" means full calendar months remaining in the paid term after the cancellation
effective date; partial months are not refunded. The administration fee is deducted from the
refund, not charged separately. Approved refunds return to the original payment method within
**5–10 business days**; refunds cannot go to a different card than the one that paid.

## SLA credits are not refunds

Uptime credits in [SLA and support tiers](../support/sla-support-tiers.md) are **service credits
applied to a future invoice**, not cash refunds, and are not processed under this policy.

Policy v4.0 was announced in the October 2026 release notes and took effect on 2026-11-01;
changes require at least 30 days' notice before the next renewal.
