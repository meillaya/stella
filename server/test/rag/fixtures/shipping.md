# Shipping & Delivery

Standard shipping takes three to five business days inside the continental
United States. Express shipping arrives in one business day when ordered before
2pm local time.

## Pricing

| Service | Cost | Delivery |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | 5 USD | 3-5 business days |
| Express | 20 USD | 1 business day |
| International | 35 USD | 7-14 business days |

Delivery estimates exclude weekends and public holidays. Orders placed after the
cutoff move to the next business day.

## Tracking

Tracking numbers are emailed when the carrier scans the parcel. If tracking has
not updated for 48 hours, support can open a carrier investigation.
