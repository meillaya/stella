# Refund Policy

Meridian refunds are processed within five business days after approval. Approved
amounts are returned to the original payment method. Shipping fees are refunded
only when the return is caused by a Meridian error.

## Eligibility

You may request a refund within 30 days of purchase. Items must be unused and
returned in their original packaging. Digital licenses are refundable only before
the license key is activated.

## Exceptions

Custom enterprise contracts follow their own terms. Contact success@meridian.example
for contract-specific refund handling.

## Support workflow

To look up the requester, call getUserName(userId) and then open a ticket with the
resolved display name. The parse_retry_count helper reads the Retry-After header so
the support bot backs off correctly.
