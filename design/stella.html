Annual plans can be refunded in full within 14 days of purchase; after that, a pro-rated credit is issued minus one month, per the current policy. Refunds beyond 14 days and any pro-rated exceptions need approval from Finance Operations; Support can open the request but cannot approve it. [1][2]

Rules of the house

For every new page

Do

  • Let evidence set the hierarchy: verdict, then numbers, then cases.
  • Use mono for every number, id, cost and timestamp.
  • Show uncertainty: CIs, sample sizes, judge agreement, drift.
  • Make every row a way in — click through to the case or trace.
  • Use real empty states with the next action, not decorative illustrations.
  • Keep the chat surface calm; provenance is one line, not a dashboard.

Don't

  • No gradients, glass, glow, emoji icons or sparkle decoration.
  • No all-caps labels, 01/02/03 sequences, left color bars or pill overload.
  • No single “quality score” without its interval and sample size.
  • No fabricated fine-tune wins, customer logos, testimonials or benchmarks.
  • No warm-cream/serif default; Stella is an instrument, not stationery.
  • No color-only status; pair the dot with a word.
